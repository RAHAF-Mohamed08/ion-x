How it works

The same reasoning chemists use, in three steps.

Observe

Each electron you remove costs more energy than the last. The pattern across IE₁, IE₂, IE₃… is the atom’s fingerprint.

Detect

The algorithm divides each value by the one before it. The largest ratio marks the point where removal starts to break into the inner shell.

Explain

Electrons before the jump are valence electrons. Their count gives the group, and the size of IE₁ narrows down the period.
